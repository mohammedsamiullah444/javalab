##module 2: streams (the pipelines)
### concept explanation
**stream** = a sequence of elements supporting **functional-style operations**
**pipeline** = chain of operations: **source -> intermediate operations -> terminal operation**
-**key principle**: streams are **lazy** (operations don’t execute until terminal operation called)
### industry analogy: **assembly line in a factory**
1.**source** = raw materials enter the line
2.**intermediate operations** = each step process the materials (filter, transform)
3.**terminal operation** = final product come out
