Table → Stores related data
Row → One complete record
Column → A specific type of information/attribute
Why is it called "relational"?

Because multiple tables can be related to each other using common fields, usually a Primary Key and Foreign Key.
### module 1: what is relational database?
### concept
A **relational database** organizes data into **tables** with **rows** and **columns**, where relationships between tables are established through **keys**.
### industry analogy: **excel on global scale**
-**spreadsheet** = single table (good for small data)
-**database** = hundreds of connected spreadsheets (handles millions of rows)
-**tables** = different sheets (customers, orders, products)
-**rows** = individual records (one customer per row)
-**columns** = attributes (name, email, phone)
-**primary key** = unique identifier (like a customer id)
